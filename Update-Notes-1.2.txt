NEW
15 engines in total: Velvet Keys, Spectral Bloom and Breath Texture join the
palette. 288 tones and 92 musical Biomes plus Blank — 64 more tones and 16 more
Biomes than 1.1. All six tracks remain freely assignable.

32-step note sequences per track, with rests, velocity, gate and chance.

Four chord journeys with pace and track selection, plus Off.

REFINED
Softer contours, high-partial control and curated balances across the voices.
A repeatable damped Rings strike reduces unintended equal-velocity accents.
The shared granular bus gains filtered Air noise and smoother transitions;
the shared room is more diffuse. Grain controls affect the ONE shared cloud;
Mix/Mixer sends determine how much each of the six tracks feeds it.

Parameter-responsive organic backgrounds leave more space at sparse settings.
Clearer antialiased lettering; sequence pitch stems, note names and velocity
bars. Hold function buttons for contextual help. Display remains 240 x 240.

Timing, gate release, long-note recording, browser data validation and timer
rollover fixes are included. FULL/ECO admission limits remain for dense patches.
