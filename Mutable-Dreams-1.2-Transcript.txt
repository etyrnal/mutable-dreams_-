Shaped by listening
Thank you for exploring Mutable Dreams, and for telling us what could feel better. Your feedback is shaping version one point two.

A gentler palette
Velvet Keys, Spectral, and Breath expand the palette. Existing voices have been refined too, with softer contours and more carefully balanced Biomes. Struck resonators now respond more consistently to equal touches, while keeping their dynamics.

Make the phrase yours
Compose up to thirty-two steps per track. Shape notes, rests, velocity, gate, and chance. Record from the keyboard, or generate a phrase and make it your own. Chord journeys give the harmony a gentle direction.

Room to breathe
The shared granular cloud gains a little air. Separate track sends let you blend each voice into it. Improved grain transitions and a more diffuse room help the sounds settle together.

A living interface
The landscapes now respond to your settings, leaving space when the music is sparse. Brighter lettering and clearer sequence views make editing easier. Hold a function button whenever you need guidance.

Keep exploring
Save your own Biomes, and keep exploring. With gratitude to Émilie Gillet, Mutable Instruments, and the open-source community. Thank you for helping this little instrument grow.
